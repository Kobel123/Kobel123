### Kobel: you decide what your AI may do with your files

Kobel is a desktop app for Windows and macOS. It sits between your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT and others) and the files on your computer.
<br clear="left">

For every file or folder, you choose one of five levels:

| Level | What the AI can do |
|---|---|
| Edit | change the file |
| Edit with backup | change the file, Kobel keeps a copy of the old version |
| Edit a copy | work on a copy, your original stays untouched |
| Read only | look, but not change |
| Hidden | the AI does not see it at all |

Kobel runs a local MCP server. Everything stays on your computer. There is no cloud, and an activity log shows what the AI did.
